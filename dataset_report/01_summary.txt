  IMPORTANT — Data quality concern:
    Most classes have only 3–15 real (non-augmented) crops.
    The vast majority of images are topup_/aug_ copies of those few originals.
    A naive random split is therefore LEAKY — augmented copies of the same
    original appear in both train and valid, inflating reported accuracy.
    For CATA-Net we will rebuild the dataset from raw video tracks to avoid this.
